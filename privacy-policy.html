Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Qinaat Digital Service ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our WhatsApp automated assistant service ("Smart Support Assistant" / "the Service").

1. Who We Are

Qinaat Digital Service is a registered individual business (NTN: 1410184985541) based in Hangu, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. We provide a WhatsApp-based AI assistant to answer questions and provide information to users who message our WhatsApp Business number.

2. Information We Collect

When you send a message to our WhatsApp Business number, we may collect and process the following information:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • The content of the messages you send to us
  • The date and time of your messages
  • Basic conversation history, used only to maintain context within an ongoing conversation

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your questions and messages
  • Provide relevant information about our services (or, where applicable, madrasa-related information)
  • Improve the quality and accuracy of our automated responses

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing to third parties, or any purpose unrelated to responding to your inquiry.

4. How Your Messages Are Processed

Messages you send are processed using WhatsApp's official Business Platform (provided by Meta) and an AI language model service (Groq) to generate helpful responses. Message content is sent securely to these services solely for the purpose of generating a reply and is not used by us for any other purpose.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Conversation history is temporarily kept in memory to allow the assistant to understand context within a conversation. This data is not permanently stored on external servers and is cleared when the system restarts. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Sensitive or Personal Matters

For questions involving sensitive, personal, or complex matters that require human judgment, our assistant is designed to direct you to contact the business owner directly rather than provide an automated response.

7. Your Rights

You may stop using the Service at any time simply by not messaging our WhatsApp number. You may also request that we delete any conversation data associated with your number by contacting us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
